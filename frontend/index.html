Safety Guidance

Flood Safety

Avoid walking or driving through floodwater. Move to higher ground and follow emergency instructions.

Earthquake Safety

Drop, cover, and hold on during shaking. Stay away from windows and damaged buildings.

Emergency Kit

Keep water, food, medication, a flashlight, phone charger, and important documents ready.

Landslide Safety

Stay away from steep slopes and areas showing signs of ground movement. Watch for falling rocks, cracks in the ground, and unusual sounds. Move to a safer location and follow local evacuation instructions.

Fire Safety

Evacuate immediately when a fire is detected. Stay low if there is smoke, use stairs instead of elevators, and never re-enter a burning building. Call emergency services from a safe location.
